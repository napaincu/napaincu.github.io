**【Agentic AI Campus & Domain Innovation Application Competition | Information Session & Empowerment Workshop】**

Designed to solve real-world campus and domain challenges using Agentic AI, this competition invites high school and university students nationwide to build intelligent applications starting from user needs. Projects should feature active reasoning, planning, tool invocation, system integration, and adaptive execution based on outcomes. Final deliverables must be operable, evaluable, controllable, and verifiable in real-world settings.

The competition features two tracks—"Campus Self-Defined Track" and "Domain-Specified Track"—covering four main themes: Smart Academic & Learning Support, Campus Administration & Service Workflows, Library & Research Knowledge Services, and Industry & Public Domain Co-Creation.

📅 **Date & Time:** Friday, September 18, 2026 | 14:00 – 16:00

📍 **Location:** Room 3F13, College of Business Building, Sanxia Campus, National Taipei University
