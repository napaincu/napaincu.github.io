## About the Competition

The 2026 WildBot Robot Challenge centers on the intelligent control and autonomous navigation of robot vehicles, inviting student teams from universities nationwide to take on autonomous navigation tasks in real-world settings—from algorithm design to controlling physical vehicles.

The competition is organized by the Ministry of Digital Affairs and executed by the Taiwan Academic-Industry Collaborative Development Association. Co-organizers include Ministry of Education AI talent-cultivation programs, among others. With its focus on Physical AI and robotics, the competition aligns closely with the talent-cultivation goals of our program.

## Key Schedule

- Registration: 2026/02/09 – 2026/05/01
- Workshop: 2026/04/19
- Preliminary Round: 2026/05/27
- Final Round & Award Ceremony: 2026/05/28

## Eligibility

Currently enrolled university and graduate students nationwide; teams of 1–5 members (cross-department and cross-school allowed), each with one faculty advisor.

## Prizes

The top three teams received cash prizes, certificates, and hardware prizes such as laptops and peripherals.
