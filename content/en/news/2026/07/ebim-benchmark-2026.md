The **EBiM Benchmark** (Embodied Bimanual Manipulation Benchmark) is a globally coordinated robotics benchmark initiative, organized by a multidisciplinary committee spanning four continents, including Universität Hamburg, Carnegie Mellon University, and Tsinghua University. Its goal is to close the gap between laboratory benchmarks and real-world robot deployment, with a focus on sim-to-real transfer.

- Official website: https://ebim-benchmark.github.io/
- Eligibility: open to academic and industry teams worldwide — no prior experience with a specific hardware platform required
- Competition hardware: Mobile FR3 Duo bimanual robot (provided by Franka Robotics)

## Four Key Research Themes

- Sim-to-Real Transfer
- Cross-Site Reproducibility
- Foundation & VLA Model Evaluation
- Contact-Rich Manipulation

## Three Competition Tasks

1. Cable Routing
2. Deformable Material Handling — Heat Pad Placement
3. Assistive Living & Feeding

Phase II real-robot validation will take place at sites across four continents: Hamburg, Munich, Pittsburgh, and Shanghai.

## Prizes

Each of the three tasks is awarded independently, with prizes worth up to **$5,250** per task:

- 1st place: $1,500 cash + Franka Robotics voucher (worth $3,750) + trophy
- 2nd place: $1,000 cash + voucher (worth $2,500)
- 3rd place: $500 cash + voucher (worth $1,250)

## How to Participate

Register via the Competition page on the official website, and join the GitHub and Discord communities for the latest updates and technical support. The simulation phase submission deadline is **August 3, 2026** — interested teams are encouraged to sign up soon!
