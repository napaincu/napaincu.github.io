## 2. Course Highlights

In this hands-on workshop, we will guide you through a complete robot development workflow — from simulation-based training to real-world robot deployment. You will simulate robots in Isaac Sim™, train policies in Isaac Lab, validate trained skills through software-in-the-loop testing, and deploy them to a physical robot.

Through practical exercises, you will gain end-to-end hands-on experience with techniques for closing the sim-to-real gap, including synthetic data generation, policy training and refinement, hardware testing on edge devices, and deployment to real robots. The workshop demonstrates how simulation accelerates robot learning and reduces the time and cost of bringing AI automation into production.

In this workshop you will learn to:

- Train robot policies and behaviors using simulation-based workflows
- Validate trained policies through software-in-the-loop (SIL) and hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testing methodologies
- Apply sim-to-real techniques to reduce the performance gap between simulated validation and physical robots
- Deploy trained models to a physical robot and execute real tasks

> Note: The course uses a collaborative setup — each workstation (computer and robot) is shared by two participants.

### Prerequisites

- Basic understanding of world foundation models
- Experience with robotics or simulation workflows (e.g., using simulation tools or working with physics-based assets)
- Familiarity with packages, virtual environments, and basic scripting
- Familiarity with 3D content concepts such as meshes, materials, transforms, and scene hierarchies from Blender, Maya, or similar DCC applications
