## 1. Event Information

- **Title**: [NAPAI Summer Camp] Robotics Hands-On Workshop II
- **Date & Time**: Tuesday, August 25, 2026, 09:45 - 17:00 (registration opens at 09:30)
- **Venue**: Room 65104, New Building, Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, National Cheng Kung University (No. 1, University Road, East District, Tainan City)
- **Hosts**: Prof. Wen-Yu Su (NCKU CSIE) and Prof. Tse-Yu Pan (NTUST AI Institute)
- **Hands-On Setup**: Physical gripper car robot and AI PC lab environment (please bring your own laptop)
- **Fee**: Free registration with a complimentary lunch

## 2. Workshop Highlights

This workshop is co-hosted by Prof. Wen-Yu Su from NCKU CSIE and Prof. Tse-Yu Pan from NTUST AI Institute, with instruction led by the champion team of a robotics challenge. Participants will work with the program-procured physical "gripper car" robot and gain hands-on experience ranging from ROS2 core control and Docker/Foxglove cross-platform connectivity to sensor integration with LiDAR and depth cameras.

Through the full hands-on workflow, participants will practice physical robot development, debugging, and sensor integration in an AI PC environment, building practical skills that can be applied directly to research and project development.
