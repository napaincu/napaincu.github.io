## 1. Course Information

- **Title**: Deploying Imitation Learning across Virtual and Physical Platforms: A Guide to Data Collection and Testing
- **Date & Time**: Thursday, August 20, 2026, 13:00 – 17:00
- **Venue**: ideaNCU Innovation Hub, 4F, Teaching & Research Building, National Central University
- **Instructor**: Prof. Tse-Yu Pan, Assistant Professor, Graduate Institute of AI Interdisciplinary Technology, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology
- **Fee**: Free
- **Audience**: Faculty, postdoctoral researchers, and outstanding graduate students from universities working on AI robotics, embodied intelligence, or digital twins (priority given to participants of the 114-2 SIGRobot reading group)

## 2. Registration

- **Deadline**: From now until Thursday, August 6, 2026 (registration closes early if seats fill up)
- **Selection**: Seats are limited to 60. The organizers will review applications after the deadline and send an "Acceptance Confirmation & Pre-course Preparation Notice" by Tuesday, August 11. Applicants who do not receive the notice have not been selected
- **Online registration**: https://forms.gle/NXYPcp1sKCtzHESN9
